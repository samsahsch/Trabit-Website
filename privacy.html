Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trabit ("Trabit", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Trabit app for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch (the "App") and the website trabit.app (the "Website"). By using the App or the Website, you acknowledge this Policy.

In short: Trabit has no servers, so we never receive your habits, health data, or coach conversations. Your App data stays on your device. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising. Some features send data to Apple, and, only if you set one up, to an AI service you choose. This Policy explains each case.

1. Who is responsible

Trabit is responsible for the App and the Website. For any privacy question or request, contact support@trabit.app.

2. Information the App handles

Depending on the features you use, the App handles:

  • Habit and activity data: habits, logs, goals, streaks, badges, training plans, and notes you enter.
  • Health and fitness data: data you allow the App to read from Apple Health. Section 5 lists every type.
  • Food data: meals, protein and calorie estimates, scanned food barcodes, pantry items, your shopping list, and dinner ideas.
  • Photos you choose: photos of meals, receipts, or your fridge or cupboard that you capture or share with the App, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Coach data: messages you send to the coach, its replies, your training plans, what you tell it about yourself, and what the coach remembers about you.
  • Names (optional): the coach asks for a first name to call you, and Friends asks for a display name that your friends see. Neither has to be your real name.
  • Social data (optional): your display name, profile photo, share code, friend connections, the goals and competitions you choose to share, cheers, and badge news.
  • Approximate location (optional): if you turn on weather awareness, a rough location (to about a kilometre) is used to look up the forecast.
  • Calendar busy times (optional): if you turn on calendar awareness, the App works out how many hours are booked on each day. Section 9 explains what it reads.
  • AI service settings (optional): if you connect your own AI service, its address, the model you picked, your sharing choices, and your API key.

The App does not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts, or payment details, and it never uses your location except for the optional weather lookup described in Section 9.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Habits, logs, goals, coach conversations, coach memory, training plans, your pantry, and everything else you enter are stored in the App on your device. The App does not sync them through iCloud or anywhere else, so your iPhone and your iPad each keep their own separate data.
  • Apple Watch, widgets, and notifications. Your paired Apple Watch gets what it shows straight from your iPhone. A small summary of your day is shared between the App, its widgets, and its Watch app on your own devices so they can show your progress.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, App data is included in that backup, as it is for other apps. Backups are handled by Apple or kept on your computer, not by us.
  • Friends. If you use friend features, the data listed in Section 6 is stored in Apple's public CloudKit database so the friends you connect with can see it. Apart from device backups, this is the only App data stored in iCloud.
  • Keys for AI services. If you connect your own AI service, its API key is stored in your device's Keychain, on that device only. It is not synced and is never sent to us.

We do not own or operate any server that receives or stores your App data.

4. The coach and AI

Apple Intelligence

By default, the App's AI features run on Apple Intelligence, through Apple's Foundation Models framework, together with Apple's on-device text recognition. This covers the coach, natural language logging, meal estimates, receipt and pantry scanning, dinner ideas, briefings, Siri, and notifications. Most requests are processed on your device by Apple's on-device model.

On iOS 27, the Enhanced coach can also use Apple Private Cloud Compute. Its requests carry the context the coach needs to answer, which can include your habits, summaries of your Apple Health data, and, if you turned them on, your calendar's busy hours and the weather forecast. Apple states that data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfil the request and is not stored or made accessible to Apple. You can turn the Enhanced coach off in Profile, in the coach settings. We do not receive any of this data.

The coach and meal estimates require a device that supports Apple Intelligence. Meal photo estimates and fridge or cupboard scans also require iOS 27.

AI services you connect (optional)

You can also connect your own account with another AI service and use it for the coach: Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, any service with an OpenAI-compatible API, or a model running on your own computer or server. This is optional and off until you set it up under Models in the Coach tab.

  • Your account and your key. You use your own account and API key with that service. The key stays in your device's Keychain. Requests go straight from your device to the service you picked. Trabit has no servers in between and stores none of it. OpenRouter passes your requests on to the model provider you choose there.
  • Only coach chat and planning. A connected model only answers what you write in the Coach tab, including training plans and weekly reviews you ask for there. Briefings, logging, meal and pantry scans, dinner ideas, Siri, and notifications always stay with Apple Intelligence.
  • Consent first. Before you use a cloud model, the App shows what it will send and to whom, and asks you to agree.
  • What is sent. To answer, the App sends the service your message, your earlier messages with that model, and the data you allow it to see.
  • You choose what each model sees. For each model and each kind of data, you pick Off, Summary (plain words, no numbers), or Full. The kinds are: habits and goals; workouts and training; sleep; heart and readiness; weight and body; food and water; cycle; what the coach remembers; and friends and competitions. Your first name and what you have told the coach about yourself are only sent if you turn that on.
  • Health data starts off. For a cloud model, habits and goals start at Full, what the coach remembers starts at Summary, and everything else starts Off, including every kind of Apple Health data and your friends. Habits that come from Apple Health, such as steps or running distance, follow the matching Health switch. If you ask about something your chat model is not allowed to see, the question goes to a private model instead: Apple Intelligence or a model on your own server.
  • Your own server. A model on your own computer or home network, or a server you mark as yours, counts as private. It can see every kind of data by default, and health questions can go to it. You are responsible for keeping that server secure.
  • The provider's policy applies. What you send is handled by that service under its own terms and privacy policy, not ours. See, for example, the privacy policies of Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. Some services may keep what you send or use it to improve their models, depending on your plan and settings, so check before you connect one.
  • Removing a model. Remove a model under Models in the Coach tab and its key is deleted from your device. What you already sent stays with that service under its policy.

5. Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • What the App can read. Only the types you allow. Depending on the features you turn on, the App can ask to read: steps; walking and running distance; cycling distance; swimming distance; flights climbed; active energy; resting (basal) energy; exercise minutes; stand minutes; workouts; sleep; mindful minutes; heart rate; resting heart rate; walking heart rate average; heart rate variability; respiratory rate; blood oxygen; VO₂ max; sleeping wrist temperature; weight; body fat percentage; lean body mass; body mass index; dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, total fat, fiber, and water; menstrual flow, only if you turn on cycle awareness; and your date of birth and sex.
  • Why. To fill in your habits (for example, distance into your Running habit, or workouts as their own habits), to work out your readiness, sleep, and strain scores, and to give the coach context. Your date of birth sets your recommended amount of sleep, and your sex adjusts the heart rate weighting in the strain score.
  • What the App writes. Nothing, unless you turn on Save meals you log to Apple Health in Profile, Health Integration. It is off by default. When on, the App writes dietary protein and dietary energy from meals you log, and nothing else.
  • When Health data leaves your device. Apart from your own device backups, only in these cases: when the Enhanced coach uses Private Cloud Compute (Section 4); when you allow an AI service you connect to see a kind of Health data (Section 4); and when a goal or competition you share with friends counts a habit filled in from Apple Health, such as steps or running distance, in which case its progress is shared with those friends (Section 6). The App asks before it shares a habit that comes from Apple Health with friends.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never sold or disclosed to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

6. Friends and competitions

Friend features are optional and require iCloud. When you use them, the following is stored in Apple's public CloudKit database:

  • Your profile: your display name, your profile photo if you add one, your share code, and your overall streak.
  • Connections: friend requests and the friends you connect with.
  • What you share: goals and competitions you choose to share, with their names, icons, targets, progress, streaks, which recent days you completed, and whether they are done.
  • Cheers and badge news: cheers you send, and, if you turn on badge news, the badges you earn.

Your Friends display name and photo are the name and photo in your Profile. When you are signed in to iCloud, the App saves them with your share code to the public database once you set them or open the Friends tab.

Progress from Apple Health. A shared goal or competition can count a habit that Apple Health fills in, such as steps or running distance. Its progress, for example your step count or distance, is then visible to the friends in it. The App asks before it shares a habit that comes from Apple Health. Your other Apple Health data, raw logs, coach conversations, and photos other than your profile photo are never shared.

You choose what to share, one goal or competition at a time, and you can stop sharing at any time. The public database is stored by Apple and is not end-to-end encrypted. The Trabit app on other people's devices can read it: it shows your profile to people you connect with or who look up your share code, and your shared goals only to the friends you share them with. You can delete your Friends profile and all of your records in the public database from within the App.

7. Photos and the share extension

Photos you capture for meal, receipt, or fridge scanning are processed by Apple Intelligence as described in Section 4. They are not sent to an AI service you connect. Photos or text you send to Trabit from the iOS share sheet are saved in your own App data for you to review. We do not receive them.

8. How we use information

Information is used only to provide the App's features to you: tracking habits, showing progress, generating coach suggestions, enabling the social features you turn on, and sending your requests to an AI service if you connect one. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling for third parties, or tracking across apps and websites.

9. Third parties

The App relies on services provided by Apple Inc. (iCloud and CloudKit, HealthKit, Apple Intelligence, Private Cloud Compute, and WeatherKit), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent personal information.

AI services you connect. If you connect one, the App sends your coach requests directly to it, as described in Section 4, and that service's privacy policy applies.

Weather. Weather awareness is off by default. If you turn it on, the App gets your approximate location (to about a kilometre) and sends it to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast from Apple Weather. The location is kept in memory on your device for up to three hours and is never stored or sent to us. The Enhanced coach may include the forecast, not your location, in its requests to Private Cloud Compute. You can turn weather awareness off in Profile, under What the coach can see, or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

Calendar. Calendar awareness is off by default. If you turn it on, the App looks only at when your events start and end, whether they were cancelled, and whether you declined them, to work out how busy each day is. It does not use event titles, notes, places, or who else is invited. The busy hours per day are used on your device to keep hard sessions off packed days, and the Enhanced coach may include them in its requests to Private Cloud Compute. They are not shared with an AI service you connect.

Food barcodes. When you scan a food barcode, the App looks the product up in Open Food Facts, an open, non-profit food database. Only the barcode number is sent. No identifiers, health data, or other information about you are included. Open Food Facts may, like any website, see your IP address when the request is made.

The App includes an optional donation link. If you choose to donate, the payment is handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy. We receive only what PayPal shares with recipients of a payment.

10. The Website

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses GoatCounter to count page views. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. The Website sets no cookies. If you choose a light or dark theme, that preference is stored in your browser only. As with any website, the hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages securely.

11. Retention and deletion

Your App data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete habits and logs in the App, and deleting the App removes its data from that device. Device backups are kept according to your backup settings. To remove what you share with friends, stop sharing the relevant goals, or delete your Friends profile and all of your public records from within the App. Removing a connected AI service deletes its key from your device; anything already sent to it is kept under that service's policy. You can export your habits as a CSV file, or all of your data as a JSON file, from Profile.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because your data is held on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For data you sent to an AI service you connected, contact that service. For anything else, email support@trabit.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

13. Security

Your data is protected by your device's security, and API keys for AI services you connect are kept in the device Keychain. What you share with friends is protected by Apple's iCloud security measures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the App's release notes or on this page.
