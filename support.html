Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Trabit?

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later. The Watch app needs a paired Apple Watch. The coach and meal estimates need a device that supports Apple Intelligence, and meal photo estimates and fridge or cupboard scans also need iOS 27. Habits, Apple Health, widgets, the Watch app, friends, and receipt scanning work without Apple Intelligence. Trabit is free, with no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. The app is available in English, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), and Spanish. Some coach messages are still in English only.

What does the coach do?

It plans your week and keeps it realistic. Tell it about a race, a concert, or an exam, and it shapes the days around it. It moves a missed session, lightens the load when your recovery is low, switches to home or hotel workouts when you travel, and suggests fewer sessions when a routine isn't sticking. It works for training, practice, and study. Nothing changes unless you accept it.

Can I use Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini with the coach?

Yes, if you want to. The coach runs on Apple Intelligence, and you can also connect your own account with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, any OpenAI-compatible service, or a model on your own computer. Set it up under Models in the Coach tab. Your API key stays in your device's Keychain, and requests go straight from your device to that service. You choose what each model can see, and Apple Health data starts off for cloud models. Only Coach chat and planning use it. Everything else stays with Apple Intelligence. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I log quickly?

Type naturally, like "ran 5km, 2L water", in the app, on your Apple Watch, in a notification reply, or from the Share sheet. Widgets tick a habit off or open the log sheet, and you can add the "Log next habit" control to Control Center or the Action Button. Anything Trabit isn't sure about waits in "For you to confirm" on the Today screen.

What can I ask Siri?

Say "Log 100 pushups on Trabit" or "Log running in Trabit", or say "Tell Trabit" and then what you did. You can also ask "What's my readiness in Trabit", "How did I sleep in Trabit", or "What's my strain in Trabit".

How do meal estimates work?

Describe your meal and Trabit gives a rough protein and calorie estimate. This needs Apple Intelligence. On iOS 27 you can also snap a photo. Treat estimates as a starting point, and enter the real numbers if you know them.

How does the pantry work?

Photograph a grocery receipt and Trabit adds the items to your pantry, each marked Plenty, Some, Low, or Out. On iOS 27 you can also snap your fridge or cupboard. In the evening you get up to three dinner ideas made from what you have, sized to the protein you still need today. Tap Cooked it and the pantry updates. Anything running low goes on your shopping list.

What are streak freezes?

A freeze covers one missed day so your streak survives. Every streak starts with one, you earn another after 7 days in a row, and you can hold up to 2. Missing two days in a row still resets the streak. Rest days never count as misses.

How do I connect with friends?

Each user gets a share code (like TRABIT-XXXXXX). Share it by QR code, link, or text. Friends see your display name, your profile photo if you add one, and the goals and competitions you choose to share. If a shared goal counts steps or distance from Apple Health, friends see that progress, and Trabit asks before sharing it. Friend features need iCloud.

An invite link opened in Safari instead of the app. What do I do?

Make sure Trabit is installed and up to date. Then long-press the link and choose Open in Trabit.

What does the Apple Watch app do?

It shows today's progress and your next habit, and you can tap to log, even when your iPhone is locked. You can also check your weekly goals, friends, and competitions, and add Trabit complications to your watch face.

How does Apple Health integration work?

With your permission, Trabit reads data like steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, and weight to track habits, work out your readiness, sleep, and strain scores, and inform the coach. Workouts from your Apple Watch appear as their own habits, like Running, Cycling, or Swimming. Writing meals back to Apple Health (protein and energy only) is optional and off by default. The Privacy Policy lists every type Trabit can read.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Trabit doesn't sync your habits, logs, goals, or coach through iCloud, so your iPhone and iPad each keep their own data. Only Friends uses iCloud, for what you share with friends. We don't run servers that hold your data. The coach uses Apple's on-device model or Apple Private Cloud Compute, unless you connect your own AI service. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. In Profile, export your habits as a CSV file, or everything as a JSON file. Delete habits and logs in the app, and deleting the app removes its data from that device. You can also delete your Friends profile and everything you've shared with friends from within the app.
